# Research Sources and Evidence

## Source hierarchy

Use sources in this order:

1. Official game website;
2. Official Steam, Xbox, PlayStation, Epic, GOG or other storefront;
3. Official Wiki, official news/community and official YouTube;
4. Reputable media;
5. Steam discussions, Reddit, community guides and player videos.

Google is a discovery channel. A search result or snippet is not final evidence. Open the original page before citing it.

## What each source can support

- Official site/storefront: identity, developer, publisher, platform listing, release state, Demo, official media and current store facts;
- Official Wiki: named mechanics, resources, buildings and systems;
- Official video: only what is visibly demonstrated, unless a transcript is actually obtained;
- Player video/community post: player language, practical example, route, layout or lead for further verification;
- Reputable secondary source: context or corroboration, not a substitute for an available official source.

Community evidence must be labeled as community evidence. It cannot override an official platform label or become an official feature merely because a player uses it.

## Evidence states

Use exactly one state for every page or material cluster:

| State | Meaning | Publication rule |
|---|---|---|
| 已交叉验证 | Multiple reliable sources agree, or one authoritative official source is sufficient | May publish with links and dates when dynamic |
| 单一官方来源 | One official page supports the claim | May publish with source link and recheck note |
| 社区线索待复核 | Useful player lead or example | Do not state as a settled official fact |
| 暂缓发布 | Evidence is weak, stale or likely misleading | Keep the gap visible; do not expand |

For every public fact, record source type, URL, collection date when dynamic, and whether it is confirmed or an inference.

## High-risk claims

Require extra checking before publication:

- Online multiplayer, co-op and cross-play;
- Game Pass, console and platform scope;
- Active codes, cheats, debug mode and downloads;
- Exact recipes, costs, drop rates, caps and progression numbers;
- Release date, Early Access, updates, prices, reviews, language support and current platform status;
- Whether a video is official or has a complete transcript.

When evidence is missing, write “暂无可靠公开资料” or “待复核”. Never invent a URL, recipe, number, transcript or platform conclusion.

## YouTube limits

- Record a real public watch URL, title, channel, visible date and intended use;
- Do not claim a complete transcript unless one was actually obtained;
- If only the title, description, chapters or visible page was inspected, say so;
- Do not use an unrelated video to fill a source slot;
- Use the YouTube keyframe Skill when a precise frame or timestamp is needed.

## Source rejection

Do not keep a source merely to fill a quota if it is:

- 404, redirected to nowhere or inaccessible;
- Suspicious SEO, third-party download or cheat site;
- A competitor page being used as final content evidence;
- A search-results page used as though it were an original source;
- A community claim that conflicts with an authoritative official label.

## Dynamic fact rule

Release state, updates, prices, reviews, languages, platform availability, Game Pass and similar facts must include a collection date and a pre-publication recheck item.
