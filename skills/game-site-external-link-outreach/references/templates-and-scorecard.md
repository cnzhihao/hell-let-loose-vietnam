# 外链研究与外展模板

本文件只在需要写具体外展稿、评分候选或建立记录时读取。所有模板都要替换成真实事实，不能把示例中的占位符原样发出。

## 1. 渠道评分卡

每项 0–2 分：

| 维度 | 0 分 | 1 分 | 2 分 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 真实受众 | 看不到真实读者 | 有读者但主题较宽 | 明确的游戏玩家/专业读者 |
| 主题相关性 | 无直接关系 | 可勉强关联 | 页面问题与目标 Wiki 页直接匹配 |
| 编辑可信度 | SEO 服务/站群/低质目录 | 规则不清或质量不稳定 | 有编辑、社区或数据库维护规则 |
| 权限可行性 | 需绕过/猜测/付费 | 需要登录或审核 | 有合法公开入口且权限明确 |
| 可测量性 | 无页面或无法核验 | 只能看到发送结果 | 有公开 URL、状态页或引荐数据 |

硬性排除项优先于总分：付费买链、链接交换、批量自动化、猜测联系方式、冒充官方、没有真实页面或读者。

## 2. 官方资源列表邮件

```text
Subject: Independent [GAME] reference site for your community resources

Hi [name/team],

I maintain [SITE], an independent, unofficial [GAME] reference site. It uses [OFFICIAL SOURCES] as primary sources and separates confirmed mechanics from community examples. It currently focuses on [RELEVANT PLAYER QUESTIONS].

If you maintain a community resources list and think it would help players, would you consider including it? No link exchange or endorsement is expected; please ignore this message if it is not a fit.

Thanks,
[name]
```

发送前核验收件人确实来自官网或官方公开页面。对方未公开收录前，记录为“资源收录询问”，不是 backlink。

## 3. 创作者感谢/补充阅读邮件

```text
Subject: Your [GAME] video cited in our independent Wiki

Hi [creator],

Thanks for sharing your [VIDEO/TOPIC] video. We cited it as a community example while preparing our independent, unofficial [GAME] Wiki. The related reference page is [TARGET PAGE].

We kept the official [OFFICIAL SOURCE] as the source for confirmed mechanics and credited your video for the practical example. If you have a moment, I would appreciate any feedback on clarity or corrections. There is no obligation to share or link to the site.

Best,
[name]
```

只提供与对方视频真正相关的一个或少量页面；不要把视频内容扩大成没有核验的机制结论。

## 4. 媒体或 Newsletter 邮件

```text
Subject: A small player resource for your [GAME] coverage

Hi [name],

I enjoyed your [ARTICLE/NEWSLETTER] about [SPECIFIC TOPIC]. I maintain [SITE], an independent [GAME] reference site built from [SOURCES]. The page most relevant to your coverage is [TARGET PAGE].

If you update the story or cover the game again, it may be useful as optional reader background. No link, endorsement, or response is required.

Thanks,
[name]
```

正文要提到对方的真实内容，不能群发相同话术。发送成功不等于媒体已经引用。

## 5. 社区问题型回答

```text
[先用 2–5 句直接回答玩家问题，并标出版本/证据边界。]

For the full step-by-step explanation, see [ONE RELEVANT PAGE].
```

不单独发宣传帖，不在多个旧帖粘贴相同链接。先确认版规、登录状态和回复权限。

## 6. 游戏数据库 Related Site 描述

```text
Title: Independent [GAME] Community Wiki

Description: Independent, unofficial [GAME] community Wiki with source-checked guides and reference pages for [TOPICS]. Not affiliated with [DEVELOPER/PUBLISHER].

URL: [SITE]

Proof: [OFFICIAL GAME PAGE]; [OFFICIAL STOREFRONT]; [OFFICIAL WIKI/NEWS]; [SITE EDITORIAL POLICY]
```

只在数据库允许此类贡献且条目确实需要该资料时提交。Pending 期间不计算公开外链；审核后必须打开公开条目再次核验。
