# SEO External-Action Boundary

Keep these actions read-only or draft-only until the exact action is explicitly authorized:

- Reddit, Steam, Discord, forums, comments and social posts;
- Directory/profile submissions or third-party Wiki/database edits;
- Contacting developers, creators, site owners or media;
- Paid links, ads, sponsorships, purchases or account creation;
- Public profile changes;
- GSC/Bing sitemap submissions and indexing requests when not explicitly requested.

Before the final publish/submit/send click after authorization, show the exact destination, content, URL, account context and external state change. Respect standing exclusions such as “do not post Reddit links”.
